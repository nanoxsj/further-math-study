3.1 Motion of a projectile

Further Mechanics · projectile motion · trajectory · range

CIE 9231 Paper 3 Further Mechanics Chapter 1，依据 Jean-Paul Muscat 与 Sophie Goldie 的 Further Mechanics 教材。
重要本章考纲要求

考生需要能够：

  1. 在合理建模假设下，把 projectile 视为只受重力作用的 particle；
  2. 分别处理水平与竖直方向的匀加速运动；
  3. 求任意时刻的位置、速度、speed 和 direction；
  4. 求最大高度、飞行时间与水平射程；
  5. 推导并使用 projectile 的 Cartesian equation of trajectory；
  6. 处理不同初始高度、碰撞、同一时刻到达及未知初速/仰角的问题。

学习路线

1. 教材知识点

1.1 Modelling assumptions

本章采用的 projectile model 是：

  • projectile 是 particle，不考虑大小与自转；
  • projectile 没有 engine 或其他持续动力；
  • air resistance、wind 等空气效应可以忽略；
  • 重力加速度恒定，教材取 \(g=10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)。

因此 acceleration 始终竖直向下：

\[ \mathbf a=\begin{pmatrix}0\\-g\end{pmatrix}. \]

1.2 Standard equations

若 projectile 从原点以 speed \(u\)、仰角 \(\alpha\) 发射，则

\[ u_x=u\cos\alpha,\qquad u_y=u\sin\alpha. \]

水平与竖直运动分别给出

\[ \boxed{x=ut\cos\alpha},\qquad \boxed{y=ut\sin\alpha-\frac12gt^2}, \]

以及

\[ \boxed{v_x=u\cos\alpha},\qquad \boxed{v_y=u\sin\alpha-gt}. \]

若初始点为 \((x_0,y_0)\)，只需改写为

\[ x=x_0+ut\cos\alpha,\qquad y=y_0+ut\sin\alpha-\frac12gt^2. \]

1.3 Maximum height, time of flight and range

最高点满足 \(v_y=0\)。从原点水平地面发射时，若落地点仍在 \(y=0\)，则

\[ t_{\max}=\frac{u\sin\alpha}{g},\qquad H=\frac{u^2\sin^2\alpha}{2g}, \]

\[ T=\frac{2u\sin\alpha}{g},\qquad R=u\cos\alpha\,T=\frac{u^2\sin2\alpha}{g}. \]

注意：\(T=2t_{\max}\) 只适用于发射点与落地点同高的情形。

1.4 Equation of the trajectory

由 \(x=ut\cos\alpha\) 得 \(t=x/(u\cos\alpha)\)。代入 \(y\)：

\[ \boxed{y=x\tan\alpha-\frac{gx^2}{2u^2}\sec^2\alpha} \]

或

\[ \boxed{y=x\tan\alpha-\frac{gx^2}{2u^2}(1+\tan^2\alpha)}. \]

这说明在模型成立时，trajectory 是 parabola。若初始高度是 \(h\)，则在右侧加上 \(h\)。

1.5 常见解题结构

  1. 先选坐标轴并明确 \(g\) 的正负；
  2. 先在水平、竖直方向写 component equations；
  3. 用题目给出的高度、水平距离或方向条件建立方程；
  4. 保留计算器中的未四舍五入值，最后按题目要求取三位有效数字；
  5. 检查时间是否为正、轨迹是否在事件发生前已经落地，以及答案是否符合几何情境。

2. Textbook Examples

Example 1.1 · Horizontal projection from a window

一只球以 \(5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) 水平飞出离地 \(4\,\mathrm m\) 的窗口。取发射点为原点、竖直向上为正，则

\[ v_x=5,\quad v_y=-10t,\qquad x=5t,\quad y=-5t^2. \]

落地时 \(y=-4\)，所以 \(t=\sqrt{0.8}=0.894\,\mathrm s\)，水平距离

\[ \boxed{x=4.47\,\mathrm m}. \]

Example 1.2 · 初始高度与最高点

若球从 \(2.2\,\mathrm m\) 高处以竖直分量 \(12\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) 发射，则

\[ y=2.2+12t-5t^2. \]

最高点满足 \(v_y=12-10t=0\)，即 \(t=1.2\)，因此最高高度为

\[ \boxed{y=9.4\,\mathrm m}. \]

若目标高度是 \(2.2\,\mathrm m\)，球在另一时刻回到该高度；两个时刻的和为 \(2u_y/g=2.4\)。

Example 1.3 · Diving board

Sharon 从水面上方 \(3.1\,\mathrm m\) 处以 \(1.8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)、仰角 \(30^\circ\) 入水。竖直运动为

\[ y=3.1+0.9t-5t^2. \]

最高高度为 \(3.14\,\mathrm m\)；入水时 \(y=0\)，取正根得

\[ t=0.882\,\mathrm s,qquad x=(1.8\cos30^\circ)t=1.38\,\mathrm m. \]

教材结论是：若另一名儿童在水平方向 \(1.5\,\mathrm m\) 处入水，两者的水平间隔不足，存在碰撞风险。

Example 1.4 · Ceiling constraint

球从地面以 \(15\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)、仰角 \(\alpha\) 发射，恰好不碰到 \(5\,\mathrm m\) 高的天花板。最高点时

\[ 15\sin\alpha=10T,qquad 5=15T\sin\alpha-5T^2. \]

消去 \(\sin\alpha\) 得 \(T=1\)，从而 \(\sin\alpha=2/3\)，且总飞行时间为 \(2\) 秒。水平射程

\[ R=30\cos\alpha=30\sqrt{1-\frac49} =\boxed{22.4\,\mathrm m}. \]

3. 教材中标注 Cambridge International 的题目

这里只收录 Chapter 1 Exercise 中明确印有 Cambridge International 来源的题目；这些教材题的原始来源是 Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709，适合作为 projectile 技巧的基础训练。配套答案依据 9781510421806_Answers.pdf。

A particle is projected with speed \(26\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) at \(30^\circ\) below the horizontal from a point \(80\,\mathrm m\) above horizontal ground. Find the distance from the starting point after \(2.3\) s, the horizontal distance before landing, and the speed and direction immediately before landing.

Answer

Using \(u_x=26\cos30^\circ\), \(u_y=-26\sin30^\circ\):

\[ \boxed{76.5\,\mathrm m},\qquad \boxed{65.4\,\mathrm m}, \]

and immediately before impact

\[ \boxed{47.7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}},\qquad \boxed{61.8^\circ\text{ below the horizontal}}. \]
Source: Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709 Paper 52 Q6, June 2011. Textbook answer: 9781510421806_Answers.pdf, Exercise 1C.

Particle \(P\) is released from rest \(7\,\mathrm m\) above the ground. At the same instant \(Q\) is projected horizontally/at angle \(\theta\) from a point \(24\,\mathrm m\) away, with speed \(50\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) and \(\tan\theta=7/24\). Show that the particles collide.

Answer

Since \(\cos\theta=24/25\) and \(\sin\theta=7/25\), at \(x=24\) particle \(Q\) has \(t=0.5\) and

\[y_Q=50(0.5)\frac7{25}-5(0.5)^2=5.75. \]

Particle \(P\) has fallen by \(5(0.5)^2=1.25\) m, so \(y_P=7-1.25=5.75\). Hence \(\boxed{P,Q\text{ collide}}\).
Source: Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709 Paper 52 Q3, November 2009.

A particle is projected at \(60^\circ\) above the horizontal. At \(1.5\) s it is moving at \(45^\circ\) above the horizontal. Find the initial speed and the horizontal and vertical displacements then.

Answer

From \(v_y/v_x=1\),

\[ \frac{V\sin60^\circ-15}{V\cos60^\circ}=1 \Longrightarrow \boxed{V=41.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}. \]

At \(t=1.5\) s,

\[ \boxed{x=30.7\,\mathrm m},\qquad \boxed{y=42.0\,\mathrm m}. \]
Source: Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709 Paper 53 Q2, June 2015.

A ball is thrown horizontally at \(5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) from a roof. If its distance from the point of projection is \(18\,\mathrm m\), find the horizontal displacement and its speed immediately before impact.

Answer

With \(x=5t\) and \(y=5t^2\), \(x^2+y^2=18^2\) gives \(t=\sqrt{(324)/(50)}\) and hence

\[ \boxed{x=8.85\,\mathrm m},\qquad \boxed{\text{speed}=18.4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}. \]
Source: Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709 Paper 53 Q5, June 2014.

A particle with speed \(20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) passes through \((16,7)\) and returns to horizontal ground. Show that the possible values of \(\tan\theta\) are \(3/4\) and \(17/4\), then find both ranges.

Answer

Substitution in the trajectory equation gives \(\tan\theta=3/4\) or \(17/4\). The corresponding ranges are

\[ \boxed{38.4\,\mathrm m\quad\text{and}\quad17.8\,\mathrm m}. \]
Source: Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709 Paper 51 Q5, June 2010.

A ball is thrown horizontally with speed \(5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) and its distance from the point of projection when it hits the ground is \(18\,\mathrm m\). Find the trajectory equation and impact speed.

Answer

\[ \boxed{x=5t,\quad y=5t^2,\quad y=0.2x^2}, \]

and

\[ \boxed{x=8.85\,\mathrm m},\qquad \boxed{v=18.4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}. \]
Source: Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709 Paper 53 Q5, June 2014.

4. Paper 3 真题题库

以下题目从本地 9231 Paper 3 Question Papers 中筛出直接考查 projectile motion、trajectory、range、collision 或同一时刻到达的题目；答案按对应 Mark Scheme 整理。

A particle is projected with speed \(100\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) from a horizontal plane, where \(\tan\theta=4/3\). The two times at which its height is \(H\) differ by \(10\) s. Find the relevant height, then determine the velocity when it lands.

Mark-scheme answer

The vertical component is \(80\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\). The time difference between equal heights is \(2\sqrt{(u_y^2-2gH)}/g\), so the mark scheme gives

\[ \boxed{H=195\,\mathrm m}. \]

The impact velocity has magnitude \(\boxed{92.2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\) and direction \(\boxed{49.4^\circ}\) below the horizontal.
Source: Cambridge International 9231/31 and 9231/32, May/June 2021, Q7; checked against the corresponding mark scheme.

Particles \(P,Q\) are projected from the top of a cliff. \(P\) has speed \(35\sqrt2\) and \(\tan\alpha=4/3\); \(Q\) is projected one second later with \(\tan\beta=1/2\). They collide \(T\) seconds after \(Q\) starts. Set up the horizontal and vertical displacement equations and solve for the unknown speed and collision time.

Method

Use elapsed times \(T+1\) for \(P\) and \(T\) for \(Q\). Horizontal coincidence gives

\[ 35(T+1)\frac45=uT\frac2{\sqrt5}. \]

Vertical coincidence is written with the common downward gravitational term; it cancels after using the one-second delay. Solve the resulting simultaneous equations for \(u\) and \(T\).
Source: Cambridge International 9231/31 and 9231/32, May/June 2022, Q7.

For a projectile launched at speed \(40\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\), let \(H\) be the greatest height and \(T\) the corresponding time. Express \(H\) and \(T\) in terms of the projection angle and use the given relation in the paper to obtain the required numerical values.

Method

Always begin with

\[ T=\frac{40\sin\theta}{g},\qquad H=\frac{(40\sin\theta)^2}{2g}. \]

The paper’s additional condition is then substituted into these two equations; do not use the range formula unless the question explicitly asks for horizontal distance.
Source: Cambridge International 9231/31 and 9231/32, May/June 2023, Q7.

Projectile \(P\) passes through a specified point and later launches particle \(Q\) when \(P\) is at its highest point. Both land at the same point and at the same time.

Key equations and mark-scheme checkpoints

For \(P\), use the trajectory equation to find its admissible initial angle/speed. If its total flight time is \(T\), then its range is \(Tu\cos\theta\). Since \(Q\) starts at the highest point, its available flight time is \(T/2\), so its range is \((T/2)V\cos\alpha\). Equal landing points gives

\[ \boxed{V\cos\alpha=2u\cos\theta}. \]
Source: Cambridge International 9231/31 and 9231/33, October/November 2023, Q5.

A projectile is launched at angle \(\tan^{-1}2\) and has travelled \(56\) m horizontally. Use the trajectory equation at the specified points to determine the required height/parameter.

Method

With \(\tan\theta=2\), use

\[ y=2x-\frac{g x^2}{2u^2}(1+4). \]

The mark scheme requires using the equation at \(x=56\) and at the second stated horizontal position, then solving the resulting simultaneous equations.
Source: Cambridge International 9231/31, October/November 2024, Q1; checked against the official mark scheme.

A particle is projected with speed \(24\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) at angle \(\theta\) and passes through a point \(35\) m horizontally from the origin and \(10\) m above the plane. Find the possible angles and then the larger range.

Mark-scheme method

Put \(x=35\), \(y=10\) in

\[ y=x\tan\theta-\frac{g x^2}{2(24)^2}(1+\tan^2\theta). \]

This gives a quadratic in \(\tan\theta\) and hence two possible launch angles. For the larger range, use the larger root in

\[ R=\frac{24^2\sin2\theta}{g}. \]
Source: Cambridge International 9231/31 and 9231/32, May/June 2025, Q2; checked against the official mark scheme.

5. 本章检查清单

  1. 只要 air resistance 被忽略，水平 acceleration 就是 \(0\)，但竖直 acceleration 仍是 \(-g\)；
  2. “moving horizontally” 的含义是 \(v_y=0\)，不是 \(y=0\)；
  3. 求 trajectory 时，必须使用与坐标原点相符的 \(x,y\)；
  4. 同一高度的两个时刻可以由同一个 quadratic 的两个根得到；
  5. 题目若改变初始高度，不能直接套用 \(T=2u\sin\alpha/g\)；
  6. 真题中若出现 barrier、string、liquid 或 air resistance，先判断它是否已经超出本章的简单 projectile model。
