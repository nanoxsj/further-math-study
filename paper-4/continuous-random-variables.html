4.1 Continuous random variables

Further Probability & Statistics · PDFs · expectation · variance · CDFs · transformations

CIE 9231 Paper 4 Further Probability & Statistics，Continuous random variables，依据 John du Feu Further Probability & Statistics。
重要本节考纲要求

考生需要能够：

  1. 使用 continuous random variable 建立概率模型；
  2. 使用 probability density function \(f(x)\) 计算概率；
  3. 检查 PDF 的非负性和总面积为 \(1\)；
  4. 由 PDF 求 expectation、variance、standard deviation、median 和 percentiles；
  5. 由 PDF 求 cumulative distribution function \(F(x)\)，以及由 CDF 求 PDF；
  6. 求函数 \(Y=g(X)\) 的 PDF 或 CDF。

1. 核心公式

若 \(X\) 的 PDF 为 \(f(x)\)，则

\[ f(x)\ge 0,\qquad \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx=1. \]

\[ P(a\le X\le b)=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx, \qquad E(X)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x f(x)\,dx. \]

\[ E(X^2)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x^2 f(x)\,dx, \qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=E(X^2)-[E(X)]^2. \]

\[ F(x)=P(X\le x)=\int_{-\infty}^{x}f(t)\,dt, \qquad f(x)=F'(x). \]

2. 教材 Examples

以下 Examples 来自 John du Feu, Further Probability & Statistics, Chapter 1。题目保持英文，答案按教材的展开方式整理。

Example 1.1 · A piecewise PDF

The number of hours Nabilah spends each day working in her garden is modelled by the continuous random variable \(X\), with PDF

\[ f(x)= \begin{cases} kx, & 0\le x<3,\\ k(6-x), & 3\le x\le 6,\\ 0, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

(i) Find the value of \(k\).

(ii) Sketch the graph of \(f(x)\).

(iii) Find \(P(2\le X\le 5)\).

Show answer

(i)

\[ \int_0^3kx\,dx+\int_3^6k(6-x)\,dx=1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 9k=1. \]

Hence \(\boxed{k=\frac19}\).

(ii) The graph is a triangle: it rises linearly from \((0,0)\) to \((3,\frac13)\) and falls linearly to \((6,0)\).

(iii)

\[ \begin{aligned} P(2\le X\le5) &=\int_2^3\frac{x}{9}\,dx+\int_3^5\frac{6-x}{9}\,dx\\ &=\frac5{18}+\frac49=\frac{13}{18}. \end{aligned} \]

Therefore \(\boxed{P(2\le X\le5)=0.72}\) to two decimal places.

Example 1.2 · A function of a continuous random variable

The continuous random variable \(X\) has PDF

\[ f(x)= \begin{cases} kx, & 0\le x<2,\\ k(4-x), & 2\le x\le4,\\ 0, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

(i) Find \(k\).

(ii) Sketch \(y=f(x)\).

(iii) Find \(P(1\le X\le3.5)\).

The continuous random variable \(Y=X^2\).

(iv) Find \(E(Y)\).

Show answer

(i)

\[ \int_0^2kx\,dx+\int_2^4k(4-x)\,dx=1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 4k=1, \]

so \(\boxed{k=\frac14}\).

(ii) The graph is two straight-line segments: from \((0,0)\) to \((2,\frac12)\), then to \((4,0)\).

(iii)

\[ P(1\le X\le3.5)=\int_1^2\frac{x}{4}\,dx+\int_2^{3.5}\frac{4-x}{4}\,dx =0.84375. \]

Thus \(\boxed{P(1\le X\le3.5)=0.84375}\).

(iv) Since \(Y=X^2\),

\[ E(Y)=E(X^2)=\int_0^2x^2\frac{x}{4}\,dx+\int_2^4x^2\frac{4-x}{4}\,dx =\boxed{\frac{14}{3}}. \]

Example 1.3 · Linear functions and variance

The continuous random variable \(X\) has PDF

\[ f(x)= \begin{cases} \dfrac{x}{50}, & 0\le x\le10,\\ 0, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

(i) Find \(E(3X+4)\).

(ii) Find \(3E(X)+4\).

(iii) Find \(\operatorname{Var}(3X+4)\).

(iv) Verify that \(\operatorname{Var}(3X+4)=3^2\operatorname{Var}(X)\).

Show answer

\[ E(X)=\int_0^{10}x\frac{x}{50}\,dx=\frac{20}{3}, \qquad E(X^2)=\int_0^{10}x^2\frac{x}{50}\,dx=50. \]

(i)

\[ E(3X+4)=3E(X)+4=\boxed{24}. \]

(ii) \(3E(X)+4=3\cdot\frac{20}{3}+4=\boxed{24}\).

(iii)

\[ \operatorname{Var}(3X+4)=9\operatorname{Var}(X), \]

where

\[ \operatorname{Var}(X)=50-\left(\frac{20}{3}\right)^2=\frac{50}{9}. \]

Therefore \(\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(3X+4)=50}\).

(iv)

\[ 3^2\operatorname{Var}(X)=9\cdot\frac{50}{9}=50, \]

which agrees with part (iii).

Example 1.4 · Finding a CDF from a PDF

A machine saws planks of wood to a nominal length. The continuous random variable \(X\) represents the error in millimetres. Its PDF is

\[ f(x)= \begin{cases} \dfrac{10-x}{50}, & 0\le x\le10,\\ 0, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

(i) Sketch \(f(x)\).

(ii) Find the cumulative distribution function \(F(x)\).

(iii) Sketch \(F(x)\) for \(0\le x\le10\).

(iv) Find \(P(2\le X\le7)\).

(v) Find the median value of \(X\).

(vi) A customer rejects planks for which the error is greater than \(8\) mm. What percentage are rejected?

Show answer

(i) The graph is a straight line from \((0,0.2)\) to \((10,0)\).

(ii)

\[ F(x)= \begin{cases} 0, & x<0,\\ \dfrac{x}{5}-\dfrac{x^2}{100}, & 0\le x\le10,\\ 1, & x>10. \end{cases} \]

(iii) \(F(x)\) is an increasing concave-down curve from \((0,0)\) to \((10,1)\).

(iv)

\[ P(2\le X\le7)=F(7)-F(2)=0.91-0.36=\boxed{0.55}. \]

(v) Solve \(F(m)=0.5\):

\[ m^2-20m+50=0. \]

The admissible root is

\[ \boxed{m=10-5\sqrt2=2.93\text{ mm}}. \]

(vi)

\[ P(X>8)=1-F(8)=1-0.96=0.04. \]

Therefore \(\boxed{4\%}\) of the planks are rejected.

Example 1.5 · A two-part PDF and CDF

The PDF of a continuous random variable \(X\) is

\[ f(x)= \begin{cases} \dfrac{x}{24}, & 0\le x\le4,\\ \dfrac{12-x}{48}, & 4\le x\le12,\\ 0, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

(i) Sketch \(f(x)\).

(ii) Find \(F(x)\).

(iii) Sketch \(F(x)\).

Show answer

(i) The graph is linear from \(0\) to \(\frac16\) on \([0,4]\), then linear down to \(0\) at \(x=12\).

(ii)

\[ F(x)= \begin{cases} 0, & x<0,\\ \dfrac{x^2}{48}, & 0\le x\le4,\\ -\dfrac12+\dfrac{x}{4}-\dfrac{x^2}{96}, & 4\le x\le12,\\ 1, & x>12. \end{cases} \]

(iii) \(F(x)\) is continuous, increasing from \(0\) to \(1\), with the slope first increasing and then decreasing according to \(f(x)\).

Example 1.6 · Finding a PDF from a CDF

The continuous random variable \(X\) has CDF

\[ F(x)= \begin{cases} 0, & x<2,\\ \dfrac{x^2}{32}-\dfrac18, & 2\le x\le6,\\ 1, & x>6. \end{cases} \]

Find the PDF \(f(x)\).

Show answer

Differentiate on each interval:

\[ f(x)=F'(x)= \begin{cases} 0, & x<2,\\ \dfrac{x}{16}, & 2\le x\le6,\\ 0, & x>6. \end{cases} \]

Example 1.7 · The PDF and CDF of a function

A company makes metal boxes by cutting four square offcuts from a sheet of metal. The length \(L\) of an offcut, in decimetres, is modelled by

\[ f_L(l)= \begin{cases} \dfrac14(4-l^2), & 0\le l\le2,\\ 0, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

Let \(A=L^2\) be the area of a square offcut.

(i) Find the CDF of \(L\).

(ii) Derive the CDF of \(A\).

(iii) Find the PDF of \(A\).

(iv) Find the mean area.

Show answer

(i)

\[ F_L(l)= \begin{cases} 0, & l\le0,\\ \dfrac{l^2}{2}-\dfrac{l^4}{16}, & 0<l\le2,\\ 1, & l>2. \end{cases} \]

(ii) Since \(L\ge0\) and \(A=L^2\),

\[ H(a)=P(A\le a)=P(L\le\sqrt a)=F_L(\sqrt a). \]

Therefore

\[ H(a)= \begin{cases} 0, & a\le0,\\ \dfrac a2-\dfrac{a^2}{16}, & 0<a\le4,\\ 1, & a>4. \end{cases} \]

(iii)

\[ h(a)=H'(a)= \begin{cases} \dfrac12-\dfrac a8, & 0<a\le4,\\ 0, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

(iv)

\[ E(A)=\int_0^4a\left(\frac12-\frac a8\right)da =\boxed{\frac43}. \]

2021 May/June · 9231/41 and 9231/42 · Question 3

The continuous random variable \(X\) has cumulative distribution function

\[ F(x)= \begin{cases} 0, & x\le0,\\ \dfrac{x^2}{81}, & 0\le x\le9,\\ 1, & x\ge9. \end{cases} \]

(a) Find \(E(X)\).

(b) Find \(\operatorname{Var}(X)\).

The random variable \(Y\) is given by \(Y^3=X\).

(c) Find the PDF of \(Y\).

Show answer

The PDF is \(f_X(x)=F'(x)=\frac{2x}{81}\) for \(0\le x\le9\).

(a)

\[ E(X)=\int_0^9x\frac{2x}{81}\,dx=\boxed{6}. \]

(b)

\[ E(X^2)=\int_0^9x^2\frac{2x}{81}\,dx=\frac{81}{2}, \]

so

\[ \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{81}{2}-6^2=\boxed{\frac92}. \]

(c) Since \(X=Y^3\), \(x=y^3\) and \(\frac{dx}{dy}=3y^2\):

\[ f_Y(y)=f_X(y^3)\left|\frac{dx}{dy}\right| =\frac{2y^3}{81}(3y^2) =\boxed{\frac{2y^5}{27}}, \]

for \(0\le y\le\sqrt[3]{9}\), and \(f_Y(y)=0\) otherwise.
