4.5 Probability generating functions

Further Probability & Statistics · PGFs · expectation · variance

CIE 9231 Paper 4 probability generating functions, based on John du Feu Further Probability & Statistics.
重要

This page uses the original English wording of the textbook examples and Paper 4 questions. Open Show answer for the textbook solution or the matching Mark Scheme working.

1. Core results

For a discrete random variable X, G_X(t)=\operatorname{E}(t^X)=\sum_x t^x\operatorname{P}(X=x),\qquad G_X(1)=1.

\operatorname{E}(X)=G_X'(1),\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=G_X''(1)+G_X'(1)-[G_X'(1)]^2.

For independent X and Y, G_{X+Y}(t)=G_X(t)G_Y(t).

2. Textbook Examples

Example 5.1

Let X be the discrete random variable that denotes the score when a fair die is thrown. Find the probability generating function (PGF) for X.

Show answer \operatorname{P}(X=x)=\frac16\quad(x=1,2,3,4,5,6). Therefore G(t)=\operatorname{E}(t^X)=\frac16t+\frac16t^2+\frac16t^3+\frac16t^4+\frac16t^5+\frac16t^6 =\frac{t(1-t^6)}{6(1-t)}.

Example 5.2

Wei is a keen archer. He has taken part in many competitions. When aiming for the centre of the target, called the gold, he finds that he hits the gold 70% of the time. Find the PGF for X, the number of gold hits in four consecutive shots at the target.

Show answer X\sim B(4,0.7), so G(t)=\sum_{x=0}^{4}\binom4x(0.7)^x(0.3)^{4-x}t^x =0.3^4+4(0.3)^3(0.7)t+6(0.3)^2(0.7)^2t^2+4(0.3)(0.7)^3t^3+(0.7)^4t^4. Hence G(t)=(0.3+0.7t)^4.

Example 5.3

A pack of playing cards contains 52 cards, with 13 cards of each of four suits (hearts, spades, diamonds and clubs). A card is selected at random from a normal pack of cards. If it is a heart, the experiment ends, otherwise it is replaced, the pack is shuffled and another card is selected. Let X represent the number of cards selected up to and including the first heart. Find the PGF for X.

Show answer X\sim\operatorname{Geo}(\frac14), so \operatorname{P}(X=x)=\frac14\left(\frac34\right)^{x-1}\quad(x=1,2,\ldots). Thus G(t)=\sum_{x=1}^{\infty}\frac14\left(\frac34\right)^{x-1}t^x =\frac14t\left[1+\frac34t+\left(\frac34t\right)^2+\cdots\right] =\frac{t}{4-3t}.

3. Paper 4 past-paper questions

2021 May/June · 9231/41 and 9231/42 · Question 6

Tanji has a bag containing 4 red balls and 2 blue balls. He selects 3 balls at random from the bag, without replacement. The number of red balls selected by Tanji is denoted by X.

(a) Find the probability generating function G_X(t) of X. [2]

Tanji also has two coins, each biased so that the probability of obtaining a head when it is thrown is \frac14. He throws the two coins at the same time. The number of heads obtained is denoted by Y.

(b) Find the probability generating function G_Y(t) of Y. [2]

The random variable Z is the sum of the number of red balls selected by Tanji and the number of heads obtained.

(c) Find the probability generating function of Z, expressing your answer as a polynomial. [3]

(d) Use the probability generating function of Z to find \operatorname{E}(Z) and \operatorname{Var}(Z). [5]

Show answer

(a) \operatorname{P}(X=1)=\frac15,\quad \operatorname{P}(X=2)=\frac35,\quad \operatorname{P}(X=3)=\frac15, so G_X(t)=\frac15t+\frac35t^2+\frac15t^3.

(b) \operatorname{P}(Y=0)=\frac9{16},\quad \operatorname{P}(Y=1)=\frac6{16},\quad \operatorname{P}(Y=2)=\frac1{16}, so G_Y(t)=\frac9{16}+\frac6{16}t+\frac1{16}t^2.

(c) G_Z(t)=G_X(t)G_Y(t)=\frac9{80}t+\frac{33}{80}t^2+\frac{28}{80}t^3+\frac9{80}t^4+\frac1{80}t^5.

(d) G_Z'(1)=\frac1{80}(9+66+84+36+5)=2.5, G_Z''(1)=\frac1{80}(66+168+108+20)=4.525. Therefore \operatorname{E}(Z)=2.5, \operatorname{Var}(Z)=G_Z''(1)+G_Z'(1)-[G_Z'(1)]^2 =4.525+2.5-2.5^2=0.775=\frac{31}{40}.

(a) Find \operatorname{Var}(Y). [4]

2023 May/June · 9231/41 and 9231/42 · Question 5

Harry has three coins.

  • One coin is biased so that, when it is thrown, the probability of obtaining a head is \frac13.
  • The second coin is biased so that, when it is thrown, the probability of obtaining a head is \frac14.
  • The third coin is biased so that, when it is thrown, the probability of obtaining a head is \frac15.

The random variable X is the number of heads that Harry obtains when he throws all three coins together.

(a) Find the probability generating function of X. [3]

Isaac has two fair coins. The random variable Y is the number of heads that Isaac obtains when he throws both of his coins together. The random variable Z is the total number of heads obtained when Harry throws his three coins and Isaac throws his two coins.

(b) Find the probability generating function of Z, expressing your answer as a polynomial in t. [4]

(c) Use the probability generating function of Z to find \operatorname{E}(Z). [2]

Show answer

(a) G_X(t)=\left(\frac23+\frac13t\right)\left(\frac34+\frac14t\right)\left(\frac45+\frac15t\right) =\frac{24}{60}+\frac{26}{60}t+\frac9{60}t^2+\frac1{60}t^3.

(b) G_Y(t)=\frac14+\frac12t+\frac14t^2, so G_Z(t)=G_X(t)G_Y(t) =\frac1{240}\left(24+74t+85t^2+45t^3+11t^4+t^5\right).

(c) G_Z'(t)=\frac1{240}(74+170t+135t^2+44t^3+5t^4), therefore \operatorname{E}(Z)=G_Z'(1)=\frac{428}{240}=\frac{107}{60}=1.78.

The random variable X has probability generating function G_X(t) given by G_X(t)=k(1+3t+4t^2), where k is a constant.

(a) Show that \operatorname{E}(X)=\frac{11}{8}. [3]

The random variable Y has probability generating function G_Y(t) given by G_Y(t)=\frac13t^2(1+2t). The random variables X and Y are independent and Z=X+Y.

(b) Find the probability generating function of Z, expressing your answer as a polynomial in t. [2]

(c) Use your answer to part (b) to find the value of \operatorname{Var}(Z). [3]

2024 October/November · 9231/42 · Question 2

The random variable X has probability generating function G_X(t) given by G_X(t)=\frac15+pt+qt^2, where p and q are constants.

(a) Given that \operatorname{E}(X)=1.1, find the numerical value of \operatorname{Var}(X). [4]

The random variable Y has probability generating function G_Y(t) given by G_Y(t)=\frac23t\left(1+\frac12t^2\right). The random variable Z is the sum of independent observations of X and Y.

(b) Find the probability generating function of Z. [2]

(c) Find \operatorname{P}(Z=2). [1]

(d) Find the mode of Z. [1]

Show answer

(a) G_X'(t)=p+2qt,\qquad p+2q=1.1, and p+q=\frac45. Thus p=\frac12, q=\frac3{10}, and G_X''(t)=2q=\frac35. Therefore \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac35+1.1-1.1^2=0.49.

(c) \operatorname{P}(Z=2)=\frac{10}{30}=\frac13.

2022 May/June · 9231/43 · Question 3

George throws two coins, A and B, at the same time. Coin A is biased so that the probability of obtaining a head is a. Coin B is biased so that the probability of obtaining a head is b, where b< a. The probability generating function of X, the number of heads obtained by George, is G_X(t). The coefficients of t and t^2 in G_X(t) are \frac5{12} and \frac1{12} respectively.

(a) Find the value of a. [2]

The random variable Y is the sum of two independent observations of X.

(b) Find the probability generating function of Y, giving your answer as a polynomial in t. [3]

(c) Find \operatorname{Var}(Y). [3]

Show answer

(a) ab=\frac1{12} and a(1-b)+(1-a)b=\frac5{12}, giving a=\frac13\qquad\left(b=\frac14\right).

(b) G_X(t)=\frac6{12}+\frac5{12}t+\frac1{12}t^2, so G_Y(t)=\frac1{144}(36+60t+37t^2+10t^3+t^4).

Y is a discrete random variable which takes the values 0,2,4,\ldots. The probability generating function of Y is given by G_Y(t)=\frac{k}{1-at^2}.

4. 常用解题技巧

4.1 先确认 PGF 的定义

对取非负整数值的离散随机变量 X，

G_X(t)=\operatorname{E}(t^X) =\sum_x t^x\operatorname{P}(X=x).

因此 t^x 的系数就是 P(X=x)。任何合法 PGF 都满足

G_X(1)=1.

如果题目给出含未知常数的 PGF，第一步通常是代入 t=1 求归一化常数；如果系数不是非负数，不能作为概率分布。

4.2 从概率分布写 PGF

若已知概率表，直接逐项写成

G_X(t)=P(X=0)+P(X=1)t+P(X=2)t^2+\cdots.

写完后检查所有系数之和为 1。例如有限取值的随机变量，PGF 应是有限多项式；不要漏写概率为零的幂次对应的项，也不要把 P(X=x) 写成 xP(X=x)。

4.3 用导数求期望和方差

先求导，再令 t=1：

\operatorname{E}(X)=G_X'(1).

二阶导数给出 factorial moment：

G_X''(1)=\operatorname{E}[X(X-1)].

因此

\operatorname{Var}(X) =G_X''(1)+G_X'(1)-[G_X'(1)]^2.

不要把 G_X''(1) 直接当成 E(X^2)；正确关系是

E(X^2)=G_X''(1)+G_X'(1).

4.4 从 PGF 提取概率

若 PGF 已展开，直接读取系数：

P(X=k)=[t^k]G_X(t).

若 PGF 是有理式，先进行适当变形或使用二项式展开。例如

(1-at)^{-r} =\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{r+k-1}{k}a^kt^k.

若前面还有 t^m，则整体幂次会平移 m；读取 t^k 时要先减去这个平移量。

4.5 独立随机变量的和

若 X、Y independent，且 Z=X+Y，则

G_Z(t)=G_X(t)G_Y(t).

这相当于把两个概率分布做 convolution，但使用 PGF 时只需相乘。若有三个独立变量，继续写

G_{X+Y+W}(t)=G_X(t)G_Y(t)G_W(t).

乘完后若要求某个概率，读取对应系数；若只要求均值和方差，可以直接使用导数或独立和的性质，避免完整展开高次多项式。

4.6 独立和的均值与方差

若 Z=X+Y 且 independent，则

E(Z)=E(X)+E(Y), \qquad \operatorname{Var}(Z)=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\operatorname{Var}(Y).

如果题目明确要求“use the PGF of Z”，应先写 G_Z=G_XG_Y，再求导；如果只问数值，直接使用独立和的均值、方差性质通常更快。

4.7 从 G_Y(t)=[G_X(t)]^2 反求 G_X

若 Y 是两个 independent observations of X 的和，则

G_Y(t)=[G_X(t)]^2.

若 G_Y(t) 是多项式，先比较常数项、一次项和最高次项，或设

G_X(t)=a_0+a_1t+\cdots+a_rt^r

再比较系数。必须选择所有系数非负且在 t=1 时等于 1 的根，不能只进行形式上的平方根。

4.8 用因式分解识别独立试验

若 PGF 可以因式分解为

G_X(t)=\prod_{i=1}^{n}(q_i+p_it), \qquad q_i=1-p_i,

通常可解释为 n 个 independent Bernoulli trials 中成功次数的 PGF。若每个因子相同，则可识别为 binomial 结构：

G_X(t)=(q+pt)^n.

因子不同时，仍可使用乘积求均值、方差或指定概率，但不能直接称为 ordinary binomial distribution。

4.9 几何型 PGF 的展开

遇到

G_X(t)=\frac{t^m}{(a-bt)^r},

先提出 a^{-r}，写成

G_X(t)=\frac{t^m}{a^r}\left(1-\frac ba t\right)^{-r}.

再使用广义二项式展开读取概率。求 P(X>k) 时，若分布形式明显，通常先求有限项和或使用 complement；不要把 PGF 本身在 t=k 处代入，t 是生成函数变量，不是随机变量的取值。

4.10 常数和参数的确定

题目可能给出

G_X(t)=k(1+3t+4t^2)

或含有 p,q 的表达式。先使用

G_X(1)=1

得到一个方程，再使用 E(X)=G_X'(1)、某个概率系数或题目给出的其他条件求剩余参数。最后检查所有概率系数非负且总和为 1。

4.11 常见失分点与答题结构

  • 把 G_X(t) 写成 \sum x t^xP(X=x)；
  • 忘记用 G_X(1)=1 归一化；
  • 把 G_X''(1) 直接当成 E(X^2)；
  • 独立随机变量求和时没有相乘 PGF；
  • 读取系数时忽略前面的 t^m；
  • 把 G_X(k) 误当作 P(X=k)；
  • 反求平方根 PGF 时选了负系数或未归一化的结果；
  • 用独立和的方差公式时忘记独立条件；
  • 有理式展开时漏掉广义二项式系数。

推荐按“验证 G(1)=1 → 识别系数或因式结构 → 求导/相乘/展开 → 提取概率或矩 → 检查系数非负和总和为 1”的顺序作答。

4.4 Non-parametric tests
