Course directory

Choose a chapter to begin. Every page combines core knowledge, textbook examples and relevant past-paper questions.

Paper 1 · Further Pure Mathematics 1

1.1 Roots of polynomial equations

  • Roots and coefficients of quadratic, cubic and quartic equations
  • Symmetric functions, Newton sums and transformations of roots
  • Reciprocal, shifted and linearly transformed roots

1.2 Rational functions and graphs

  • Asymptotes, stationary points, ranges and discriminant methods
  • |f(x)|, f(|x|), 1/f(x) and y^2=f(x)
  • Translations, reflections and transformations of graphs

1.3 Summation of series

  • Standard results for \sum r, \sum r^2 and \sum r^3
  • Shifting, partial fractions and the method of differences
  • Finite sums, convergence and infinite series

1.4 Matrices

  • Matrix operations, zero and identity matrices
  • 2\times2 and 3\times3 determinants and inverses
  • Plane transformations, compositions and area scale factors
  • Fixed points, invariant lines and eigen-directions

1.5 Polar coordinates

  • Conversion between Cartesian and polar coordinates
  • Symmetry, the pole, initial line and radial extrema
  • Maximum distance from the initial line or a specified ray
  • Areas of regions in polar coordinates

1.6 Vectors

  • Cartesian, normal and parametric vector forms of planes
  • Intersections, parallelism and containment of lines and planes
  • Vector products, angles and lines of intersection
  • Shortest distances involving lines, planes and skew lines

1.7 Proof by induction

  • Base case, inductive hypothesis and inductive step
  • Series, recurrence sequences and inequalities
  • Higher derivatives and matrix-power proofs

Paper 2 · Further Pure Mathematics 2

2.1 Hyperbolic functions

  • Exponential and logarithmic definitions of hyperbolic functions
  • Graphs of the six hyperbolic functions
  • Core identities, double-angle and addition formulae
  • Osborn’s rule and inverse hyperbolic functions

2.2 Matrices

  • Three-variable systems and matrix equations
  • Intersections of three planes and consistency
  • Eigenvalues, eigenvectors and characteristic equations
  • Diagonalisation A=QDQ^{-1} and matrix powers

2.3 Differentiation

  • Derivatives of hyperbolic, inverse trigonometric and inverse hyperbolic functions
  • Second derivatives of implicit and parametric curves
  • Maclaurin series and implicit differentiation
  • Series approximations and order of error

2.4 Integration

  • Hyperbolic-function and standard integrals
  • Completing the square, trigonometric and hyperbolic substitutions
  • Reduction formulae and numerical integration inequalities
  • Arc length and surface area of revolution

2.5 Complex numbers

  • de Moivre’s theorem and its proof
  • nth roots of unity
  • Multiple-angle expansions of \sin^n\theta and \cos^n\theta
  • The C+iS method for sums

2.6 Differential equations

  • First-order linear equations and integrating factors
  • CF and PI for second-order constant-coefficient equations
  • Polynomial, exponential and trigonometric particular integrals
  • Simplification by substitution

Paper 3 · Further Mechanics

3.1 Motion of a projectile

  • Standard horizontal and vertical motion equations
  • Trajectory, range, maximum height and collisions

3.2 Equilibrium of a rigid body

  • Moments, couples and the rigid-body model
  • Equilibrium of forces and moments
  • Rods, ladders, laminas, friction and limiting equilibrium

3.3 Centre of mass

  • Particle systems, uniform bodies and composite centres of mass
  • Laminas, discs, semicircles, sectors and removed parts
  • Suspension, tipping and mixed equilibrium questions

3.4 Circular motion

  • Angular speed, velocity, centripetal acceleration and force
  • Horizontal circles, conical pendulums and banked tracks
  • Vertical circles, energy, tension, normal reaction and loss of contact

3.5 Hooke’s law

  • Elastic strings, springs, modulus of elasticity and extension
  • Multiple strings, elastic potential energy and work
  • Vertical elastic motion, energy equations and slack-string conditions

3.6 Linear motion under a variable force

  • Newton’s second law as a differential equation
  • Forces depending on time, displacement or velocity
  • Resistance, driving force and limiting speed

3.7 Momentum

  • Impulse, conservation of momentum and Newton’s experimental law
  • Direct impact, smooth planes, barriers and oblique impact

Paper 4 · Further Probability & Statistics

4.1 Continuous random variables

  • Probability density functions and cumulative distribution functions
  • Expectation, variance, median and percentiles
  • Functions of continuous random variables

4.2 Inference using normal and t-distributions

  • Confidence intervals, one-sample tests, two-sample tests and paired t-tests
  • Normal assumptions, t-distributions, degrees of freedom and critical values

4.3 Chi-squared tests

  • Contingency tables, independence tests and goodness-of-fit tests
  • Expected frequencies, degrees of freedom, test statistics and critical values

4.4 Non-parametric tests

  • One-sample and paired sign tests
  • Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank and rank-sum tests
  • Hypotheses about medians and non-normal data

4.5 Probability generating functions

  • PGFs, expectation, variance and sums of independent variables
  • Standard discrete distributions and coefficient methods
  • Recovering probabilities from coefficients
