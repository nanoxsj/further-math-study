2.1 Hyperbolic functions

定义 · 图像 · 恒等式 · 反双曲函数

CIE 9231 Paper 2，依据 2026–2027 syllabus 与 Collins Further Pure Mathematics 2 Chapter 1。
重要本节考纲要求

考生需要能够：

  1. 理解双曲函数 \(\sinh x\)、\(\cosh x\)、\(\tanh x\)、\(\operatorname{sech}x\)、\(\operatorname{cosech}x\)、\(\coth x\) 用指数函数给出的定义；
  2. 作出双曲函数的图像；
  3. 证明并使用双曲函数恒等式，例如 \(\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x\equiv1\)、\(\sinh2x\equiv2\sinh x\cosh x\)，以及对应于标准三角恒等式的类似结果；
  4. 理解反双曲函数的定义，推导并使用其对数形式。

学习路线

1. 教材知识点

1.1 Definitions of the hyperbolic functions

双曲正弦与双曲余弦用指数函数定义为

\[ \boxed{\sinh x=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2,\qquad x\in\mathbb R} \]

\[ \boxed{\cosh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2,\qquad x\in\mathbb R}. \]

由它们可以定义其余四个双曲函数：

\[ \tanh x=\frac{\sinh x}{\cosh x} =\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}{e^x+e^{-x}},\qquad x\in\mathbb R, \]

\[ \coth x=\frac{\cosh x}{\sinh x} =\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{e^x-e^{-x}},\qquad x\in\mathbb R,\ x\ne0, \]

\[ \operatorname{cosech}x=\frac1{\sinh x} =\frac2{e^x-e^{-x}},\qquad x\in\mathbb R,\ x\ne0, \]

\[ \operatorname{sech}x=\frac1{\cosh x} =\frac2{e^x+e^{-x}},\qquad x\in\mathbb R. \]

1.2 Graphs of the hyperbolic functions

作双曲函数图像时，常用 \(\sinh x\) 与 \(\cosh x\) 分别作为 \(e^x\) 与 \(\pm e^{-x}\) 的平均值来理解，再逐段分析奇偶性、渐近线和极值。

1.3 Identities involving hyperbolic functions

双曲函数恒等式与三角恒等式十分相似。核心恒等式为

\[ \boxed{\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1}. \]

由此可推出

\[ \operatorname{sech}^2x=1-\tanh^2x, \]

\[ \operatorname{cosech}^2x=\coth^2x-1. \]

二倍角公式：

\[ \sinh2x=2\sinh x\cosh x, \]

\[ \cosh2x=\cosh^2x+\sinh^2x=2\cosh^2x-1=2\sinh^2x+1. \]

加法公式：

\[ \cosh(A\pm B)=\cosh A\cosh B\pm\sinh A\sinh B, \]

\[ \sinh(A\pm B)=\sinh A\cosh B\pm\cosh A\sinh B, \]

\[ \tanh(A\pm B)=\frac{\tanh A\pm\tanh B}{1\pm\tanh A\tanh B}. \]

把三角恒等式转换为双曲恒等式时使用 Osborn’s rule：把三角函数换成对应的双曲函数，凡出现两个 \(\sinh\) 相乘，就把该处的正负号反转。例如由 \(\cos^2x+\sin^2x=1\) 得到 \(\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1\)。

1.4 Inverse hyperbolic functions

反双曲函数是对应双曲函数在限制定义域后的反函数，图像是原函数关于直线 \(y=x\) 的反射。考试常用对数形式：

\[ \boxed{\sinh^{-1}x=\ln\left(x+\sqrt{x^2+1}\right),\qquad x\in\mathbb R} \]

\[ \boxed{\cosh^{-1}x=\ln\left(x+\sqrt{x^2-1}\right),\qquad x\geqslant1} \]

\[ \boxed{\tanh^{-1}x=\frac12\ln\left(\frac{1+x}{1-x}\right),\qquad |x|<1.} \]

反双曲函数也可写成 \(\operatorname{arsinh}x\)、\(\operatorname{arcosh}x\)、\(\operatorname{artanh}x\) 等形式。\(\cosh x\) 必须先限制为 \(x\geqslant0\) 才是单射，因此 \(\cosh^{-1}x\) 只取非负值。

2. Textbook Examples

Example 1 · Page 4

Write \(\sinh 2\) in terms of the exponential function.

Show solution

Since

\[ \sinh x=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2, \]

putting \(x=2\) gives

\[ \boxed{\sinh 2=\frac{e^2-e^{-2}}2}. \]

Example 2 · Page 4

Find the value of \(\sinh 2\) to 3 decimal places.

Show solution

Using the hyperbolic function on a calculator, or substituting into the exponential form,

\[ \boxed{\sinh 2=3.627}\ \text{to 3 d.p.} \]

Example 3 · Page 5

Find the exact value of \(\operatorname{sech}(\ln 2)\).

Show solution

Using \(\operatorname{sech}x=\dfrac2{e^x+e^{-x}}\) with \(x=\ln2\),

\[ \operatorname{sech}(\ln2)=\frac2{2+\frac12} =\frac2{\frac52} =\boxed{\frac45}. \]

Example 4 · Page 5

Find the value(s) of \(x\), to 3 decimal places, for which \(\cosh x=2\).

Show solution

Using \(\cosh x=\dfrac{e^x+e^{-x}}2\),

\[ \frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2=2 \;\Longrightarrow\; e^{2x}-4e^x+1=0. \]

The quadratic formula gives

\[ e^x=2\pm\sqrt3=3.732\ldots\ \text{or}\ 0.267\ldots, \]

so

\[ \boxed{x=\ln(3.732\ldots)=1.317\ \text{or}\ x=\ln(0.267\ldots)=-1.317} \]

to 3 decimal places.

Example 5 · Page 6

Sketch the graph of \(f(x)=\sinh x\) for \(-5\leqslant x\leqslant5\).

Show solution

\(\sinh x\) is the mean of \(e^x\) and \(-e^{-x}\). Sketch these two curves and take their mean at each value of \(x\).

Example 6 · Page 6

Sketch the graph of \(f(x)=\cosh x\) for \(-5\leqslant x\leqslant5\).

Show solution

\(\cosh x\) is the mean of \(e^x\) and \(e^{-x}\); the curve is symmetric about the \(y\)-axis and has minimum point \((0,1)\).

Example 7 · Page 7

Sketch the graph of \(f(x)=\tanh x\) for \(-2.5\leqslant x\leqslant2.5\).

Show solution

\(y=\tanh x\) is odd and approaches the horizontal asymptotes \(y=\pm1\).

Example 8 · Page 7

Sketch the graph of \(f(x)=\coth x\) for \(-10\leqslant x\leqslant10\).

Show solution

\(y=\coth x\) is undefined at \(x=0\); the lines \(x=0\) and \(y=\pm1\) are asymptotes.

Example 9 · Page 8

Sketch the graph of \(f(x)=\operatorname{cosech}x\) for \(-10\leqslant x\leqslant10\).

Show solution

\(y=\operatorname{cosech}x\) is undefined at \(x=0\), and the lines \(x=0\) and \(y=0\) are asymptotes.

Example 10 · Page 8

Sketch the graph of \(f(x)=\operatorname{sech}x\) for \(-5\leqslant x\leqslant5\).

Show solution

\(y=\operatorname{sech}x\) has a maximum at \((0,1)\) and the line \(y=0\) is an asymptote.

Example 11 · Page 9

Determine whether the trigonometric identity \(\cos^2x+\sin^2x=1\) can be converted to \(\cosh^2x+\sinh^2x=1\) for hyperbolic functions.

Show solution

Substitute the exponential forms:

\[ \cosh^2x+\sinh^2x =\left(\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2\right)^2+\left(\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2\right)^2. \]

Expanding,

\[ \frac{e^{2x}+2+e^{-2x}}4+\frac{e^{2x}-2+e^{-2x}}4 =\frac{e^{2x}+e^{-2x}}2. \]

This is not equal to \(1\), so

\[ \boxed{\cosh^2x+\sinh^2x\neq1}. \]

The correct identity is \(\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1\).

Example 12 · Page 10

Show that \(\cosh(A+B)=\cosh A\cosh B+\sinh A\sinh B\).

Show solution

Substitute the exponential forms:

\[ \begin{aligned} \cosh A\cosh B+\sinh A\sinh B &=\left(\frac{e^A+e^{-A}}2\right)\left(\frac{e^B+e^{-B}}2\right) +\left(\frac{e^A-e^{-A}}2\right)\left(\frac{e^B-e^{-B}}2\right)\\ &=\frac{e^{A+B}+e^{A-B}+e^{-A+B}+e^{-A-B}}4\\ &\quad+\frac{e^{A+B}-e^{A-B}-e^{-A+B}+e^{-A-B}}4\\ &=\frac{2e^{A+B}+2e^{-(A+B)}}4\\ &=\frac{e^{A+B}+e^{-(A+B)}}2\\ &=\cosh(A+B). \end{aligned} \]

The related results

\[ \cosh(A-B)=\cosh A\cosh B-\sinh A\sinh B, \]

\[ \sinh(A\pm B)=\sinh A\cosh B\pm\cosh A\sinh B \]

follow in the same way.

Example 13 · Page 11

Given that \(\cosh x=3\), find the value of:

(a) \(\sinh x\);

(b) \(\tanh x\).

Show solution

(a) Using \(\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1\),

\[ 9-\sinh^2x=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \sinh^2x=8, \]

so

\[ \boxed{\sinh x=\pm2\sqrt2}. \]

(b) Using \(\tanh x=\dfrac{\sinh x}{\cosh x}\),

\[ \boxed{\tanh x=\pm\frac{2\sqrt2}3}. \]

Example 14 · Page 13

Sketch the graph and state the domain of \(y=\sinh^{-1}x\).

Show solution

The graph of \(y=\sinh^{-1}x\) is the reflection of \(y=\sinh x\) in the line \(y=x\). Since \(\sinh x\) is one-to-one on \(\mathbb R\),

\[ \boxed{\text{domain: }x\in\mathbb R}. \]

Example 15 · Page 14

State a possible domain of \(\cosh^{-1}x\) and sketch the graph for your chosen domain. What are the implications for the domain of \(y=\cosh x\) for \(y=\cosh^{-1}x\) to be a function?

Show solution

\(\cosh x\) is many-to-one, so its domain is restricted to \(x\geqslant0\). The inverse function then has

\[ \boxed{\text{domain: }x\geqslant1}. \]

Example 16 · Page 14

Write \(\sinh^{-1}x\) in logarithmic form.

Show solution

Let \(y=\sinh^{-1}x\), so \(x=\sinh y\). Then

\[ x=\frac{e^y-e^{-y}}2 \;\Longrightarrow\; e^{2y}-2xe^y-1=0. \]

The quadratic formula gives

\[ e^y=x\pm\sqrt{x^2+1}. \]

The negative sign gives a negative value of \(e^y\), which is impossible, so

\[ \boxed{\sinh^{-1}x=\ln\left(x+\sqrt{x^2+1}\right)}. \]

Example 17 · Page 15

Write \(\sinh^{-1}2\) in logarithmic form.

Show solution

Substitute \(x=2\) into \(\sinh^{-1}x=\ln\left(x+\sqrt{x^2+1}\right)\):

\[ \boxed{\sinh^{-1}2=\ln\left(2+\sqrt5\right)}. \]

2020 May/June · 9231/21 & 9231/22 · Question 5 · 11 marks

The curves \(C_1:y=\cosh x\) and \(C_2:y=\sinh 2x\) intersect at the point where \(x=a\).

(a) Find the exact value of \(a\), giving your answer in logarithmic form. [4]

(b) Sketch \(C_1\) and \(C_2\) on the same diagram. [2]

(c) Find the exact value of the length of the arc of \(C_1\) from \(x=0\) to \(x=a\). [5]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a) At the intersection, \(\cosh a=\sinh 2a=2\sinh a\cosh a\). Since \(\cosh a\neq0\),

\[ \sinh a=\frac12. \]

Using the logarithmic form of \(\sinh^{-1}\),

\[ \boxed{a=\sinh^{-1}\!\left(\frac12\right) =\ln\left(\frac12+\sqrt{\frac14+1}\right) =\ln\left(\frac12+\frac{\sqrt5}2\right)}. \]

(b) \(C_1\) is the even U-shaped curve \(\cosh x\), and \(C_2\) is the odd increasing curve \(\sinh 2x\). They intersect in the first quadrant at \(x=a=\ln\left(\frac12+\frac{\sqrt5}2\right)\) and, by symmetry, also at \(x=-a\).

(c) For \(C_1:y=\cosh x\),

\[ \frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}=\sinh x, \qquad \sqrt{1+\left(\frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}\right)^2} =\sqrt{1+\sinh^2x} =\cosh x. \]

Hence

\[ s=\int_0^a\cosh x\,\mathrm dx =\bigl[\sinh x\bigr]_0^a =\sinh a =\boxed{\frac12}. \]

Sources: Cambridge International 9231/21/M/J/2020, Q5; 9231/22/M/J/2020, Q5.

2020 May/June · 9231/23 · Question 6(a) · 3 marks

Starting from the definitions of \(\tanh\) and \(\operatorname{sech}\) in terms of exponentials, prove that

\[ 1-\tanh^2\theta=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta. \]

Show answer and mark scheme

Starting from

\[ \tanh\theta=\frac{e^\theta-e^{-\theta}}{e^\theta+e^{-\theta}},\qquad \operatorname{sech}\theta=\frac2{e^\theta+e^{-\theta}}, \]

\[ \begin{aligned} 1-\tanh^2\theta &=1-\left(\frac{e^\theta-e^{-\theta}}{e^\theta+e^{-\theta}}\right)^2\\ &=\frac{(e^\theta+e^{-\theta})^2-(e^\theta-e^{-\theta})^2}{(e^\theta+e^{-\theta})^2}\\ &=\frac4{(e^\theta+e^{-\theta})^2}\\ &=\boxed{\operatorname{sech}^2\theta}. \end{aligned} \]

Source: Cambridge International 9231/23/M/J/2020, Q6(a).

2020 October/November · 9231/21 & 9231/23 · Question 8(a)–(b) · 5 marks

(a) Sketch the graph of \(y=\coth x\) for \(x\geqslant0\) and state the equations of the asymptotes. [2]

(b) Starting from the definitions of \(\coth\) and \(\operatorname{cosech}\) in terms of exponentials, prove that

\[ \coth^2x-\operatorname{cosech}^2x=1. \]

[3]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a) For \(x\geqslant0\), \(y=\coth x\) starts at the vertical asymptote \(x=0\) and decreases towards \(y=1\).

The asymptotes are

\[ \boxed{x=0\ \text{and}\ y=1}. \]

(b) Starting from

\[ \coth x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{e^x-e^{-x}},\qquad \operatorname{cosech}x=\frac2{e^x-e^{-x}}, \]

\[ \begin{aligned} \coth^2x-\operatorname{cosech}^2x &=\left(\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{e^x-e^{-x}}\right)^2-\left(\frac2{e^x-e^{-x}}\right)^2\\ &=\frac{e^{2x}+2+e^{-2x}-4}{(e^x-e^{-x})^2}\\ &=\frac{e^{2x}-2+e^{-2x}}{(e^x-e^{-x})^2}\\ &=1. \end{aligned} \]

Sources: Cambridge International 9231/21/O/N/2020, Q8(a)–(b); 9231/23/O/N/2020, Q8(a)–(b).

2021 May/June · 9231/21 & 9231/22 · Question 7 · 10 marks

It is given that

\[ y=\operatorname{sech}^{-1}\left(x+\frac12\right). \]

(a) Express \(\cosh y\) in terms of \(x\) and hence show that

\[ \sinh y\,\frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}=-\frac1{\left(x+\frac12\right)^2}. \]

[3]

(b) Find the first three terms in the Maclaurin’s series for \(\operatorname{sech}^{-1}\left(x+\frac12\right)\) in the form

\[ \ln a+bx+cx^2, \]

where \(a\), \(b\) and \(c\) are constants to be determined. [7]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a) Since \(\operatorname{sech}y=x+\dfrac12\),

\[ \boxed{\cosh y=\left(x+\frac12\right)^{-1}}. \]

Differentiating \(\operatorname{sech}y=x+\dfrac12\) with respect to \(x\),

\[ -\operatorname{sech}y\tanh y\,\frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}=1. \]

Using \(\operatorname{sech}y\tanh y=\dfrac{\sinh y}{\cosh^2y}=\sinh y\left(\operatorname{sech}y\right)^2\),

\[ \sinh y\,\frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}=-\left(\operatorname{sech}y\right)^2 =\boxed{-\frac1{\left(x+\frac12\right)^2}}. \]

(b) At \(x=0\),

\[ y(0)=\operatorname{sech}^{-1}\!\left(\frac12\right) =\cosh^{-1}2=\ln\left(2+\sqrt3\right). \]

From part (a),

\[ y'(0)=-\frac1{\left(\frac12\right)^2\sinh y(0)} =-\frac4{\sinh(\cosh^{-1}2)} =-\frac4{\sqrt3}. \]

Differentiating

\[ \sinh y\,y'=-\left(x+\frac12\right)^{-2} \]

gives, at \(x=0\),

\[ \sinh y\,y''+\cosh y\,(y')^2=2\left(x+\frac12\right)^{-3} \;\Longrightarrow\; \sqrt3\,y''(0)+\frac{16}{3}=16, \]

so \(y''(0)=\dfrac{16}{3\sqrt3}\). Hence

\[ \boxed{y=\ln\left(2+\sqrt3\right)-\frac4{\sqrt3}x+\frac8{3\sqrt3}x^2}. \]

Sources: Cambridge International 9231/21/M/J/2021, Q7; 9231/22/M/J/2021, Q7.

2021 May/June · 9231/23 · Question 6(a) · 3 marks

Starting from the definitions of \(\sinh\) and \(\cosh\) in terms of exponentials, prove that

\[ 2\sinh2x=\cosh2x-1. \]

Show answer and mark scheme

Using

\[ \sinh2x=\frac{e^{2x}-e^{-2x}}2,\qquad \cosh2x=\frac{e^{2x}+e^{-2x}}2, \]

\[ \begin{aligned} 2\sinh2x &=2\left(\frac{e^{2x}-e^{-2x}}2\right)\\ &=e^{2x}-e^{-2x}\\ &=\frac{2e^{2x}-2e^{-2x}}2\\ &=\frac{e^{2x}+e^{-2x}}2-1\\ &=\boxed{\cosh2x-1}. \end{aligned} \]

Starting from the definition of \(\cosh\) in terms of exponentials, prove that

2022 May/June · 9231/21 & 9231/22 · Question 2 · 8 marks

(a) Starting from the definitions of \(\cosh\) and \(\sinh\) in terms of exponentials, prove that

\[ \cosh2x=2\sinh^2x+1. \]

[3]

(b) Find the set of values of \(k\) for which

\[ \cosh2x=k\sinh x \]

has two distinct real roots. [5]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a) Using \(\cosh x=\dfrac{e^x+e^{-x}}2\) and \(\sinh x=\dfrac{e^x-e^{-x}}2\),

\[ \begin{aligned} 2\sinh^2x+1 &=2\left(\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2\right)^2+1\\ &=\frac{e^{2x}-2+e^{-2x}}2+1\\ &=\frac{e^{2x}+e^{-2x}}2\\ &=\boxed{\cosh2x}. \end{aligned} \]

(b) Using part (a),

\[ 2\sinh^2x+1=k\sinh x \;\Longrightarrow\; 2\sinh^2x-k\sinh x+1=0. \]

This is a quadratic in \(\sinh x\). It has two distinct real roots when its discriminant is positive:

\[ k^2-8>0. \]

Therefore

\[ \boxed{k<-\sqrt8\ \text{or}\ k>\sqrt8}. \]

Sources: Cambridge International 9231/21/M/J/2022, Q2; 9231/22/M/J/2022, Q2.

Starting from the definitions of \(\cosh\) and \(\sinh\) in terms of exponentials, prove that

(a) Find the exact values of \(x\) for which

\[ \cosh 2x=6\sinh^2x, \]

giving your answers in logarithmic form. [4]

(b) Sketch the curves \(C_1:y=\cosh 2x\) and \(C_2:y=6\sinh^2x\) on the same diagram. [2]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a) Using \(\cosh2x=1+2\sinh^2x\),

\[ 1+2\sinh^2x=6\sinh^2x \;\Longrightarrow\; 4\sinh^2x=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \sinh x=\pm\frac12. \]

Therefore

\[ \boxed{x=\pm\ln\left(\frac12+\frac{\sqrt5}2\right)}. \]

(b) \(C_1:y=\cosh2x\) is even with minimum \((0,1)\); \(C_2:y=6\sinh^2x\) is even, passes through the origin and increases rapidly. The curves intersect at

\[ x=\pm\ln\left(\frac12+\frac{\sqrt5}2\right), \]

with \(C_1\) above \(C_2\) between these points and below it outside.
