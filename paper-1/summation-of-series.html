1.3 Summation of series

标准求和公式 · 错位相减 · 裂项消项 · 无穷和

CIE 9231 Paper 1，依据 2026–2027 syllabus 与 Further Pure Mathematics 1 Chapter 2.1–2.3。
重要本节考纲要求

考生需要能够：

  1. 使用 \(\sum r\)、\(\sum r^2\)、\(\sum r^3\) 的标准结果求相关级数；
  2. 使用 method of differences 求有限级数之和，必要时先作部分分式分解；
  3. 直接考察前 \(n\) 项和，判断级数是否收敛，并在收敛时求无穷和。

学习路线

1. 教材知识点

1.1 Sequences, series and sigma notation

数列是按顺序排列的数；级数是数列各项的和。若通项为 \(u_r\)，前 \(n\) 项和写作

\[ S_n=\sum_{r=1}^{n}u_r. \]

上下限决定所包含的项。若求和式中没有 \(r\)，仍需按上下限重复相加，例如

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}c=nc. \]

若已知前 \(n\) 项和，则

\[ u_1=S_1,\qquad u_r=S_r-S_{r-1}\quad(r\ge2). \]

1.2 Standard results

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}r=\frac12n(n+1), \]

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}r^2=\frac16n(n+1)(2n+1), \]

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}r^3=\frac14n^2(n+1)^2. \]

使用时先展开通项，再利用求和的线性性质。不要把 \(\sum r^2\) 误写成 \(\left(\sum r\right)^2\)。

1.3 Changing limits

对于连续的一段整数，可用“大和减小和”：

\[ \sum_{r=a}^{b}u_r =\sum_{r=1}^{b}u_r-\sum_{r=1}^{a-1}u_r. \]

同理，若 \(S_n=\sum_{r=1}^{n}u_r\)，则

\[ \sum_{r=n+1}^{2n}u_r=S_{2n}-S_n. \]

1.4 Method of differences

核心是把通项写成相邻项之差：

\[ u_r=f(r)-f(r+1). \]

于是

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}u_r =[f(1)-f(2)]+[f(2)-f(3)]+\cdots+[f(n)-f(n+1)], \]

中间项全部消去，只剩

\[ \boxed{S_n=f(1)-f(n+1)}. \]

若裂项跨度大于 1，通常会留下多于一个首项和尾项，必须将前几项与最后几项完整写出。

1.5 Sum to infinity

先求有限前 \(n\) 项和 \(S_n\)，再直接考察

\[ \lim_{n\to\infty}S_n. \]

若极限存在且有限，则级数收敛，且该极限就是无穷和。若 \(u_n\not\to0\)，级数一定不收敛；但 \(u_n\to0\) 本身不能保证收敛。

2. Textbook Examples

Example 2.1 · Pages 43–44

For the series

\[ 2+5+8+\cdots+500, \]

(i) find a formula for the \(r\)th term \(u_r\); (ii) find the number of terms; (iii) find the sum by reversing and adding; (iv) confirm the answer using sigma notation.

Show solution

The sequence has common difference \(3\), so

\[ u_r=3r-1. \]

Since \(3r-1=500\), there are \(167\) terms. Reversing and adding gives

\[ 2S=167(2+500), \]

so

\[ \boxed{S=41917}. \]

Also

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{167}(3r-1) =3\cdot\frac{167\cdot168}{2}-167 =41917. \]

Example 2.2 · Page 44

Calculate the sum of the integers from \(100\) to \(200\) inclusive.

Show solution

\[ \sum_{r=100}^{200}r =\sum_{r=1}^{200}r-\sum_{r=1}^{99}r =\frac{200\cdot201}{2}-\frac{99\cdot100}{2}. \]

Hence

\[ \boxed{15150}. \]

Example 2.3 · Page 47

Given \(u_r=r^2+2r-1\):

  1. write down the first three terms;
  2. find \(\sum_{r=1}^{n}u_r\);
  3. check the result for \(n=3\).
Show solution

The first three terms are \(2,7,14\). Also

\[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{r=1}^{n}(r^2+2r-1) &=\sum r^2+2\sum r-\sum1\\ &=\frac16n(n+1)(2n+1)+n(n+1)-n\\ &=\boxed{\frac16n(2n^2+9n+1)}. \end{aligned} \]

For \(n=3\), the formula gives \(23\), and \(2+7+14=23\).

Example 2.4 · Page 48

Write

\[ (1\times3)+(2\times4)+(3\times5)+\cdots+n(n+2) \]

using sigma notation, and hence find its sum.

Show solution

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}r(r+2) =\sum_{r=1}^{n}(r^2+2r). \]

Therefore

\[ \boxed{\sum_{r=1}^{n}r(r+2)=\frac16n(n+1)(2n+7)}. \]

Example 2.5 · Page 50

Calculate

\[ 5+10+20+40+\cdots+2560+5120. \]

Show solution

Let the sum be \(S\). Then

\[ \begin{aligned} S&=5+10+20+\cdots+2560+5120,\\ 2S&=10+20+40+\cdots+5120+10240. \end{aligned} \]

Subtracting gives \(S=10240-5\), hence

\[ \boxed{S=10235}. \]

Example 2.6 · Pages 50–51

(i) Show that

\[ \frac1r-\frac1{r+1}=\frac1{r(r+1)}. \]

(ii) Hence find

\[ \frac1{1\times2}+\frac1{2\times3}+\cdots+\frac1{30\times31}. \]

Show solution

The identity follows by combining the two fractions. Therefore

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{30}\frac1{r(r+1)} =\sum_{r=1}^{30}\left(\frac1r-\frac1{r+1}\right) =1-\frac1{31}. \]

Hence the sum is

\[ \boxed{\frac{30}{31}}. \]

Example 2.7 · Page 52

(i) Show that \((2r+1)^2-(2r-1)^2=8r\).

(ii) Hence find \(\sum_{r=1}^{n}8r\).

(iii) Deduce the standard result for \(\sum_{r=1}^{n}r\).

Show solution

Expanding proves the identity. Summing gives

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}8r =(3^2-1^2)+(5^2-3^2)+\cdots+[(2n+1)^2-(2n-1)^2]. \]

Thus

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}8r=(2n+1)^2-1=4n^2+4n, \]

and hence

\[ \boxed{\sum_{r=1}^{n}r=\frac12n(n+1)}. \]

Example 2.8 · Pages 52–53

(i) Show that

\[ \frac2r-\frac3{r+1}+\frac1{r+2} =\frac{r+4}{r(r+1)(r+2)}. \]

(ii) Hence find

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}\frac{r+4}{r(r+1)(r+2)}. \]

(iii) Deduce the sum to infinity.

Show solution

After using the stated decomposition and writing the first and last terms, all middle terms cancel, leaving

\[ S_n=\frac32-\frac2{n+1}+\frac1{n+2} =\frac{n(3n+7)}{2(n+1)(n+2)}. \]

Therefore

\[ \boxed{S_\infty=\frac32}. \]

3. Cambridge examination questions in the textbook

Exercise 2B, Question 11

It is given that

\[ S_n=\sum_{r=1}^{n}u_r=2n^2+n. \]

Write down \(S_1,S_2,S_3,S_4\). Express \(u_r\) in terms of \(r\), justifying your answer. Find

\[ \sum_{r=n+1}^{2n}u_r. \]

Show solution

\[ S_1=3,\quad S_2=10,\quad S_3=21,\quad S_4=36. \]

For \(r\ge2\),

\[ u_r=S_r-S_{r-1}=4r-1, \]

and this also gives \(u_1=3\). Finally,

\[ \sum_{r=n+1}^{2n}u_r=S_{2n}-S_n =\boxed{6n^2+n}. \]

Source: Cambridge International 9231 Paper 11, Question 3, October/November 2013.

Exercise 2C, Question 11

Given that

\[ u_k=\frac1{2k-1}-\frac1{2k+1}, \]

express \(\sum_{k=13}^{n}u_k\) in terms of \(n\) and deduce \(\sum_{k=13}^{\infty}u_k\).

Show solution

\[ \sum_{k=13}^{n}u_k =\left(\frac1{25}-\frac1{27}\right)+\cdots+ \left(\frac1{2n-1}-\frac1{2n+1}\right) =\boxed{\frac1{25}-\frac1{2n+1}}. \]

Hence

\[ \boxed{\sum_{k=13}^{\infty}u_k=\frac1{25}}. \]

Source: Cambridge International 9231 Paper 11, Question 1, October/November 2014.

Exercise 2C, Question 12

For positive integers \(n\),

\[ a_n=\frac{n+5}{n^2-n+1}-\frac{n+6}{n^2+n+1}, \]

and \(S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n\). Find \(S_{30}\) correct to 3 decimal places and the least \(N\) for which \(S_N>4.9\).

Show solution

Successive terms telescope because the second fraction in \(a_n\) is the first fraction in \(a_{n+1}\). Thus

\[ S_N=6-\frac{N+6}{N^2+N+1}. \]

Therefore

\[ S_{30}=6-\frac{36}{931}=\boxed{5.961}\quad\text{(3 d.p.)}. \]

Checking successive values gives \(S_2<4.9\) and \(S_3>4.9\), so

\[ \boxed{N=3}. \]

Source: Cambridge International 9231 Paper 11, Question 4, October/November 2015.

Exercise 2C, Question 13

Let \(f(r)=r(r+1)(r+2)\).

  1. Show that \(f(r)-f(r-1)=3r(r+1)\).
  2. Hence show that \(\sum_{r=1}^{n}r(r+1)=\frac13n(n+1)(n+2)\).
  3. Using the standard result for \(\sum r\), deduce the standard result for \(\sum r^2\).
  4. Find the sum

\[ 1^2+2(2^2)+3^2+2(4^2)+\cdots+2(n-1)^2+n^2, \]

where \(n\) is odd.

Show solution

Summing \(f(r)-f(r-1)=3r(r+1)\) gives

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}r(r+1)=\frac13n(n+1)(n+2). \]

Since \(r(r+1)=r^2+r\),

\[ \sum r^2=\frac13n(n+1)(n+2)-\frac12n(n+1) =\boxed{\frac16n(n+1)(2n+1)}. \]

For the final series, add one extra copy of every even square to the usual sum of squares. With \(m=(n-1)/2\),

\[ \boxed{\frac16n(n+1)(2n+1)+\frac23m(m+1)(2m+1)}. \]

Source: Cambridge International 9231 Paper 11, Question 4, October/November 2012.

2019 May/June · 9231/11 & 9231/12 · Question 2 · 5 marks

Let

\[ u_n=\frac{4\sin\left(n-\frac12\right)\sin\frac12}{\cos(2n-1)+\cos1}. \]

(i) Show that \(u_n=\sec n-\sec(n-1)\). [2]

(ii) Use the method of differences to find \(\sum_{n=1}^{N}u_n\). [2]

(iii) Explain why \(u_1+u_2+u_3+\cdots\) does not converge. [1]

Show answer and mark scheme

(i) Using the sum-to-product identities,

\[ \cos(2n-1)+\cos1=2\cos n\cos(n-1), \]

and

\[ 2\sin\left(n-\frac12\right)\sin\frac12 =\cos(n-1)-\cos n. \]

Therefore

\[ u_n =\frac{2\{\cos(n-1)-\cos n\}}{2\cos n\cos(n-1)} =\boxed{\sec n-\sec(n-1)}. \]

(ii) Writing the first few and final terms,

\[ \sum_{n=1}^{N}u_n =(\sec1-1)+(\sec2-\sec1)+\cdots+(\sec N-\sec(N-1)) =\boxed{\sec N-1}. \]

(iii) The sequence \(\sec N\) does not approach a finite limit as \(N\to\infty\); in particular, \(\cos N\) oscillates and can be arbitrarily close to \(0\). Hence the sequence of partial sums \(\sec N-1\) has no limit, so the infinite series diverges.

Sources: Cambridge International 9231/11/M/J/2019, Q2; 9231/12/M/J/2019, Q2.

2019 October/November · 9231/11, 9231/12 & 9231/13 · Question 5 · 9 marks

Let

\[ S_N=\sum_{r=1}^{N}(5r+1)(5r+6), \qquad T_N=\sum_{r=1}^{N}\frac1{(5r+1)(5r+6)}. \]

(i) Use standard results to show that

\[ S_N=\frac13N(25N^2+90N+83). \]

[3]

(ii) Use the method of differences to express \(T_N\) in terms of \(N\). [4]

(iii) Find \(\lim_{N\to\infty}\dfrac{S_NT_N}{N^3}\). [2]

Show answer and mark scheme

(i) Expanding the summand,

\[ S_N=\sum_{r=1}^{N}(25r^2+35r+6) =25\sum_{r=1}^{N}r^2+35\sum_{r=1}^{N}r+6N. \]

Substituting the standard results,

\[ S_N =\frac{25}{6}N(N+1)(2N+1) +\frac{35}{2}N(N+1)+6N \]

\[ =\boxed{\frac13N(25N^2+90N+83)}. \]

(ii) Partial fractions give

\[ \frac1{(5r+1)(5r+6)} =\frac15\left(\frac1{5r+1}-\frac1{5r+6}\right), \]

Hence

\[ T_N=\frac15\left[ \left(\frac16-\frac1{11}\right) +\left(\frac1{11}-\frac1{16}\right) +\cdots +\left(\frac1{5N+1}-\frac1{5N+6}\right) \right]. \]

All intermediate terms cancel, so

\[ \boxed{T_N=\frac1{30}-\frac1{5(5N+6)}}. \]

(iii) From the leading terms,

\[ \frac{S_N}{N^3}\longrightarrow\frac{25}{3}, \qquad T_N\longrightarrow\frac1{30}. \]

Therefore

\[ \boxed{\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{S_NT_N}{N^3}=\frac5{18}}. \]

Sources: Cambridge International 9231/11/O/N/2019, Q5; 9231/12/O/N/2019, Q5; 9231/13/O/N/2019, Q5.

2020 May/June · 9231/11 & 9231/12 · Question 4 · 10 marks

(a) By first expressing \(1/(r^2-1)\) in partial fractions, show that

\[ \sum_{r=2}^{n}\frac1{r^2-1} =\frac34-\frac{2n+1}{2n(n+1)}. \]

[5]

(b) Deduce \(\sum_{r=2}^{3}\frac1{r^2-1}\). [1]

(c) Find \(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{r=n+1}^{2n}\frac1{r^2-1}\). [4]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a) Since \(r^2-1=(r-1)(r+1)\),

\[ \frac1{r^2-1} =\frac12\left(\frac1{r-1}-\frac1{r+1}\right). \]

Therefore

\[ \sum_{r=2}^{n}\frac1{r^2-1} =\frac12\left[ \left(1-\frac13\right) +\left(\frac12-\frac14\right) +\left(\frac13-\frac15\right) +\cdots +\left(\frac1{n-1}-\frac1{n+1}\right) \right] \]

\[ =\frac12\left(1+\frac12-\frac1n-\frac1{n+1}\right) =\frac34-\frac{2n+1}{2n(n+1)}, \]

as required.

(b) Put \(n=3\) in part (a):

\[ \sum_{r=2}^{3}\frac1{r^2-1}=\boxed{\frac{11}{24}}. \]

(c) Let \(F(n)=\sum_{r=2}^{n}1/(r^2-1)\). Then

\[ \sum_{r=n+1}^{2n}\frac1{r^2-1}=F(2n)-F(n). \]

Using part (a),

\[ F(2n)-F(n) =\left(\frac34-\frac{4n+1}{4n(2n+1)}\right) -\left(\frac34-\frac{2n+1}{2n(n+1)}\right). \]

Both remaining fractions tend to \(0\), so

\[ \boxed{0}. \]

Sources: Cambridge International 9231/11/M/J/2020, Q4; 9231/12/M/J/2020, Q4.

2020 May/June · 9231/13 · Question 3 · 8 marks

Let

\[ S_n=2^2+6^2+10^2+\cdots+(4n-2)^2. \]

(a) Use standard results to show that

\[ S_n=\frac43n(4n^2-1). \]

[4]

(b) Express \(n/S_n\) in partial fractions and find \(\sum_{n=1}^{N}n/S_n\). [4]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a)

\[ S_n=\sum_{r=1}^{n}(4r-2)^2 =\sum_{r=1}^{n}(16r^2-16r+4). \]

Hence

\[ S_n =\frac{16}{6}n(n+1)(2n+1)-8n(n+1)+4n \]

\[ =\frac43n(4n^2-1), \]

as required.

(b) Therefore

\[ \frac{n}{S_n}=\frac3{4(2n-1)(2n+1)} =\frac38\left(\frac1{2n-1}-\frac1{2n+1}\right). \]

Writing the terms explicitly,

\[ \sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{n}{S_n} =\frac38\left[ \left(1-\frac13\right) +\left(\frac13-\frac15\right) +\cdots +\left(\frac1{2N-1}-\frac1{2N+1}\right) \right]. \]

Thus

\[ \boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{n}{S_n}=\frac38\left(1-\frac1{2N+1}\right) =\frac{3N}{4(2N+1)}}. \]

Source: Cambridge International 9231/13/M/J/2020, Q3.

(b) Use the method of differences to find

Hence

\[ =\frac17\left(\frac18-\frac1{7n+8}\right), \]

so

2020 October/November · 9231/12 · Question 3 · 7 marks

(a) By simplifying

\[ (x^n-\sqrt{x^{2n}+1})(x^n+\sqrt{x^{2n}+1}), \]

show that

\[ \frac1{x^n-\sqrt{x^{2n}+1}}=-x^n-\sqrt{x^{2n}+1}. \]

[1]

Let

\[ u_n=x^{n+1}+\sqrt{x^{2n+2}+1} +\frac1{x^n-\sqrt{x^{2n}+1}}. \]

(b) Use the method of differences to find \(\sum_{n=1}^{N}u_n\). [3]

(c) Deduce the values of \(x\) for which the infinite series converges and give its sum. [3]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a) The product is a difference of two squares:

\[ (x^n-\sqrt{x^{2n}+1})(x^n+\sqrt{x^{2n}+1}) =x^{2n}-(x^{2n}+1)=-1. \]

Dividing by \(x^n-\sqrt{x^{2n}+1}\) gives the stated identity.

(b) Using part (a),

\[ u_n=\left(x^{n+1}+\sqrt{x^{2n+2}+1}\right) -\left(x^n+\sqrt{x^{2n}+1}\right). \]

Thus the sum is

\[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{n=1}^{N}u_n ={}&\left(x^2+\sqrt{x^4+1}-x-\sqrt{x^2+1}\right)\\ &+\left(x^3+\sqrt{x^6+1}-x^2-\sqrt{x^4+1}\right)+\cdots\\ &+\left(x^{N+1}+\sqrt{x^{2N+2}+1}-x^N-\sqrt{x^{2N}+1}\right). \end{aligned} \]

All intermediate terms cancel, giving

\[ \boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{N}u_n =x^{N+1}+\sqrt{x^{2N+2}+1}-x-\sqrt{x^2+1}}. \]

(c) A finite limit requires \(x^{N+1}\to0\), which occurs precisely when \(|x|<1\). Then

\[ \sqrt{x^{2N+2}+1}\longrightarrow1. \]

Therefore the series converges for \(-1<x<1\), and its sum is

\[ \boxed{1-x-\sqrt{x^2+1}}. \]

Source: Cambridge International 9231/12/O/N/2020, Q3.

2021 May/June · 9231/11 & 9231/12 · Question 2 · 9 marks

(a) Use standard results to find \(\sum_{r=1}^{n}(1-r-r^2)\). [3]

(b) Show that

\[ \frac{1-r-r^2}{(r^2+2r+2)(r^2+1)} =\frac{r+1}{(r+1)^2+1}-\frac r{r^2+1}, \]

and hence find the corresponding sum from \(r=1\) to \(n\). [5]

(c) Deduce the sum to infinity. [1]

Show answer and mark scheme

(a)

\[ \sum_{r=1}^{n}(1-r-r^2) =n-\frac{n(n+1)}2-\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6 \]

\[ =\boxed{-\frac13n(n^2+3n-1)}. \]

(b) Combining the fractions on the right gives

\[ \frac{(r+1)(r^2+1)-r\{(r+1)^2+1\}} {\{(r+1)^2+1\}(r^2+1)} =\frac{1-r-r^2}{(r^2+2r+2)(r^2+1)}, \]

  2. 能把多项式通项拆开并正确代入三个标准公式；
  3. 能从部分分式或恒等式识别消项结构；
  4. 能完整保留未消去的首项与尾项；
  5. 能从 \(S_n\) 的极限判断收敛并求无穷和。
