1.6 Vectors

平面与直线 · 向量积 · 角度、交点与距离

CIE 9231 Paper 1，依据 2026–2027 syllabus 与 Further Pure Mathematics 1 Chapter 7。
重要本节考纲要求

考生需要能够：

  1. 使用并互相转换平面的 Cartesian、normal 与 parametric vector forms；
  2. 计算 vector product，并理解其方向和大小；
  3. 判断直线与平面相交、平行或直线位于平面内，并求交点；
  4. 求点到平面的垂足及距离；
  5. 求直线与平面、两个平面之间的角；
  6. 求两个平面的交线；
  7. 求两条 skew lines 的最短距离及 common perpendicular 的方程。

学习路线

1. 教材知识点

1.1 Equations of a plane

过点 \(A\)，位置向量为 \(\mathbf a\)，并包含两个不平行方向 \(\mathbf b,\mathbf c\) 的平面可写成

\[ \boxed{\mathbf r=\mathbf a+\lambda\mathbf b+\mu\mathbf c}. \]

若 \(\mathbf n\) 是平面的 normal vector，则

\[ (\mathbf r-\mathbf a)\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n=0, \]

即

\[ \boxed{\mathbf r\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n=p},\; p=\mathbf a\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n. \]

若 \(\mathbf n=(a,b,c)\)，Cartesian form 为

\[ \boxed{ax+by+cz=d}. \]

从 parametric form 转换时，可用 \(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c\) 求 normal；也可消去 \(\lambda,\mu\)。

1.2 A line and a plane

设直线

\[ \mathbf r=\mathbf a+\lambda\mathbf d \]

和平面 \(\mathbf r\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n=p\)。

  • 若 \(\mathbf d\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n\ne0\)，直线与平面有唯一交点；
  • 若 \(\mathbf d\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n=0\)，直线平行于平面；
  • 在平行情形下，若 \(\mathbf a\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n=p\)，则整条直线位于平面内。

求交点时，把直线的 \(x,y,z\) 参数式代入平面方程，先求参数，再代回直线。

1.3 Foot of a perpendicular and distance to a plane

从点 \(P\) 到平面 \(\mathbf r\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n=p\) 的垂线方向为 \(\mathbf n\)。先写

\[ \mathbf r=\mathbf p_0+t\mathbf n, \]

再与平面联立，所得交点 \(M\) 是 foot of the perpendicular，距离为 \(|PM|\)。

对于平面 \(ax+by+cz=d\) 和点 \((x_0,y_0,z_0)\)，距离公式为

\[ \boxed{ D=\frac{|ax_0+by_0+cz_0-d|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}} }. \]

1.4 Angle between a line and a plane

若直线方向为 \(\mathbf d\)，平面 normal 为 \(\mathbf n\)，直线与平面的锐角为 \(\alpha\)，则

\[ \boxed{ \sin\alpha=\frac{|\mathbf d\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n|}{|\mathbf d|\,|\mathbf n|} }. \]

这里使用 \(\sin\alpha\)，因为直线与平面的角与 \(\mathbf d\)、\(\mathbf n\) 之间的角互余。

1.5 Intersection and angle of two planes

两个不平行平面的交集是一条直线。可用两种方法求交线：

  1. 令一个坐标为参数，联立两个 Cartesian equations；
  2. 取两平面的 normals \(\mathbf n_1,\mathbf n_2\)，则交线方向平行于 \[\mathbf n_1\times\mathbf n_2,\] 再求交线上的一个点。

两平面的锐角等于其 normals 的锐角：

\[ \boxed{ \cos\theta=\frac{|\mathbf n_1\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n_2|} {|\mathbf n_1|\,|\mathbf n_2|} }. \]

所有包含平面 \(\pi_1:f_1=0\) 与 \(\pi_2:f_2=0\) 的公共交线的平面可写成

\[ pf_1+qf_2=0, \]

其中 \(p,q\) 不同时为零。

1.6 Vector product

对于

\[ \mathbf a=(a_1,a_2,a_3),\; \mathbf b=(b_1,b_2,b_3), \]

\[ \boxed{ \mathbf a\times\mathbf b= \begin{pmatrix} a_2b_3-a_3b_2\\ a_3b_1-a_1b_3\\ a_1b_2-a_2b_1 \end{pmatrix} }. \]

\(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b\) 同时垂直于 \(\mathbf a\) 和 \(\mathbf b\)，方向由 right-hand rule 决定，且

\[ |\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|=|\mathbf a|\,|\mathbf b|\sin\theta. \]

因此平行四边形面积为 \(|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|\)，三角形面积为其一半。向量积满足

\[ \mathbf a\times\mathbf b=-(\mathbf b\times\mathbf a). \]

1.7 Distances involving lines

点 \(P\) 到直线 \(\mathbf r=\mathbf a+\lambda\mathbf d\) 的距离可写成

\[ \boxed{ D=\frac{|(\mathbf p-\mathbf a)\times\mathbf d|}{|\mathbf d|} }. \]

两条 skew lines

\[ \mathbf r=\mathbf a+\lambda\mathbf b,\; \mathbf r=\mathbf c+\mu\mathbf d \]

的共同垂直方向为 \(\mathbf b\times\mathbf d\)。最短距离为

\[ \boxed{ D=\frac{|(\mathbf c-\mathbf a)\boldsymbol\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf d)|} {|\mathbf b\times\mathbf d|} }. \]

若要求 common perpendicular 的方程，可分别取两线上一般点 \(P,Q\)，令 \(\overrightarrow{PQ}\) 同时垂直于 \(\mathbf b\) 和 \(\mathbf d\)，解出两个参数后用 \(P\) 或 \(Q\) 与方向 \(\mathbf b\times\mathbf d\) 写直线。

Find the equation of the plane through \(A(4,2,0)\), \(B(3,1,1)\) and \(C(4,-1,1)\).

提示Solution

Two independent directions in the plane are

\[ \overrightarrow{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\1\end{pmatrix},\; \overrightarrow{AC}=\begin{pmatrix}0\\-3\\1\end{pmatrix}. \]

Hence a parametric equation is

\[ \boxed{\mathbf r=\begin{pmatrix}4\\2\\0\end{pmatrix} +\lambda\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\1\end{pmatrix} +\mu\begin{pmatrix}0\\-3\\1\end{pmatrix}}. \]

Thus \(x=4-\lambda\), \(y=2-\lambda-3\mu\) and \(z=\lambda+\mu\). Eliminating \(\lambda\) and \(\mu\) gives

\[ \boxed{2x+y+3z=10}. \]

Write down the equation of the plane through \((2,1,3)\) whose normal is \(4\mathbf i+5\mathbf j+6\mathbf k\).

提示Solution

With \(\mathbf a=(2,1,3)\) and \(\mathbf n=(4,5,6)\),

\[ (\mathbf r-\mathbf a)\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n=0. \]

Therefore \(4(x-2)+5(y-1)+6(z-3)=0\), so

\[ \boxed{4x+5y+6z=31}. \]

\[ \mathbf r=\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\\4\end{pmatrix} +\lambda\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\-1\end{pmatrix} \]

meets the plane \(5x+y-z=1\).

提示Solution

A point of the line has coordinates \((2+\lambda,3+2\lambda,4-\lambda)\). Substitution gives

\[ 5(2+\lambda)+(3+2\lambda)-(4-\lambda)=1, \]

so \(9+8\lambda=1\) and \(\lambda=-1\). The point of intersection is

\[ \boxed{(1,1,5)}. \]

Show that the line

\[ \mathbf r=\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\0\end{pmatrix} +t\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\\-2\end{pmatrix} \]

is parallel to the plane \(2x+4y+5z=8\).

提示Solution

The direction of the line is \(\mathbf d=(3,1,-2)\) and a normal to the plane is \(\mathbf n=(2,4,5)\). Since

\[ \mathbf d\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n=3(2)+1(4)-2(5)=0, \]

the line direction is perpendicular to the normal. Hence the line is parallel to the plane.

\[ 2(5)+4(2)+5(-2)=8. \]

This point belongs to the plane. Therefore the whole line lies in the plane.

\[ \mathbf r=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\2\\-3\end{pmatrix} +t\begin{pmatrix}-1\\2\\5\end{pmatrix} \]

and the plane \(2x+3y+z=4\).

提示Solution

Take \(\mathbf n=(2,3,1)\) and \(\mathbf d=(-1,2,5)\). If \(A\) is the angle between \(\mathbf n\) and \(\mathbf d\), then

\[ \cos A=\frac{\mathbf n\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf d}{|\mathbf n||\mathbf d|} =\frac{-2+6+5}{\sqrt{14}\sqrt{30}}. \]

Hence \(A=63.95^\circ\). The angle between the line and the plane is

\[ 90^\circ-A=26.05^\circ, \]

Find the line of intersection of

\[ 3x+2y-3z=-18 \;\text{and}\; x-2y+z=12. \]

提示Solution

Putting \(y=0\) gives \(x=3\), \(z=9\), so \(P=(3,0,9)\). Putting \(z=1\) gives \(x=-1\), \(y=-6\), so \(Q=(-1,-6,1)\). Then

\[ \overrightarrow{PQ}=\begin{pmatrix}-4\\-6\\-8\end{pmatrix} =-2\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\\4\end{pmatrix}. \]

Therefore one equation of the common line is

\[ \boxed{\mathbf r=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-6\\1\end{pmatrix} +t\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\\4\end{pmatrix}}. \]

Find the acute angle between \(\pi_1:2x+3y+5z=8\) and \(\pi_2:5x+y-4z=12\).

提示Solution

The normals are \(\mathbf n_1=(2,3,5)\) and \(\mathbf n_2=(5,1,-4)\). Hence

\[ \cos\theta=\frac{\mathbf n_1\boldsymbol\cdot\mathbf n_2}{|\mathbf n_1||\mathbf n_2|} =\frac{-7}{\sqrt{38}\sqrt{42}}. \]

This gives \(\theta=100.1^\circ\) between the chosen normals. The acute angle between the planes is therefore

\[ \boxed{180^\circ-100.1^\circ=79.9^\circ}. \]

Find the plane through \((1,2,3)\) that contains the common line of

\[ \pi_1:2x+2y+z+3=0, \; \pi_2:2x+3y+z+13=0. \]

提示Solution

Every plane in the sheaf through the common line has equation

\[ p(2x+2y+z+3)+q(2x+3y+z+13)=0, \]

where \(p\) and \(q\) are not both zero. Substitution of \((1,2,3)\) gives

\[ 12p+24q=0, \]

\[ -2(2x+2y+z+3)+(2x+3y+z+13)=0, \]

\[ \boxed{2x+y+z=7}. \]
